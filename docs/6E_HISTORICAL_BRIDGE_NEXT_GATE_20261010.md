## Critical feature-provenance seam

The current historical causal projection includes `features` and `execution_features`; its quote columns contain timestamps and bid/ask prices. Those quotes cannot independently derive the complete 37-field vector's trade flow, CVD, depth, reload or absorption proxies. A matching point hash and quote timestamp therefore do not prove feature provenance.

A later adapter needs the original source-bound feature-publication/completed-event-prefix witness, or a separately reviewed and verified accepted replay proof. It must specify the exact feature schema, causal window cutoff/equality, warmup, gap/reset rules, book/trade reconstruction identity and publication ordering. The future contract must say which proofs are metadata-only and which require separately authorized source-event access. Do not invent zero-valued features to stand in for missing evidence, or call a replenishment proxy an authenticated iceberg.

Outcome fields and old selected trades are not feature inputs. Any target-free sidecar must retain its original source/projection identity and complete coverage; it must not be created by selecting favorable opportunities after reading outcomes. Complete future-input hashes may identify a whole supplied file, but future values must never influence an earlier causal publication.

## Independent constructed acceptance groups

These are proposed requirements, not passing tests:

1. **Complete metadata first.** An invalid last session, role, pin, count, disposition, path or publication identity refuses before earlier prices/features are traversed. Reject omitted/reordered sessions, skipped-prefix selection, forged train labels, reserved dates and later-role inputs outside the prospective scope. Preserve complete degraded, empty or reconstruction-only ledger dispositions; do not silently remove them.
2. **Original publication visibility.** Retain source-event/completed-quote ordinals and the first equal-time cursor. A favorable or opposing equal-time suffix must not change the earlier published vector or decision. Legitimate equal-time quotes remain ordered observations, not deduplicated rows. Event-completion semantics must be explicit, not inferred from a timestamp alone.
3. **Causal feature horizon.** Independently construct cutoff-minus-one, equality and plus-one events, warmup, gaps, resets and missing book/trade proof. Future-only events change the correct complete-input identity but not prior consumed digests/features when source/metadata identity and the consumed prefix remain unchanged. Changing an asserted source pin changes the native digest seed; distinguish that from a future-only complete-fixture pin change. A quote-only witness must fail when richer feature provenance is required.
4. **Calendar and contract identity.** Distinguish UTC source date, declared session window and exchange trading-date conventions. Test declared timezone/DST boundaries and every pinned dated-contract transition without moving the fixed research window to suit outcomes. Treat declared calendar rules as assumptions unless separately verified; do not splice or back-adjust contracts into one quote stream.
5. **One original seed.** In a later separately reviewed causal adapter, fees, realized gains/losses, reserves, cooldown and liabilities survive session/date/contract boundaries. A clock or new session does not reseed capital or make a missing margin observation fresh. Existing inventory takes precedence over a new signal. No newly computed policy may borrow a completed future roundtrip's terminal state.
6. **Absorbing UNKNOWN.** Old-contract UNKNOWN, including zero monetary commitments, blocks later dates/contracts; no late fill, cancellation, reconciliation or new accounting clock may erase it. Complete equity/return stays null. Later native input auditing can continue without claiming accounting-time advancement.
7. **Whole-cohort capacity.** Predeclare complete decision/quote/record/byte/runtime bounds and test exact/excess cases. The held admission record limit is 2,000, not a daily allowance. A legitimate complete session can exceed it; that is a capacity blocker, not permission to truncate decisions, select a convenient interval, silently shard/reseed or return a successful prefix. A larger or streaming journal needs its own reviewed semantics and complete independent verification.

All proposed cases use invented fixtures first. No historical row may be called synthetic to satisfy an existing guard. No new selector, execution delay, spread treatment, hold, cooldown, end-buffer or loss definition is optimized from known outcomes.

## Gate before a historical funded experiment

Historical execution requires more than the metadata validator. A separately reviewed historical native/accounting representation must recompute every original policy decision causally, retain complete quote/publication coverage, preserve the fixed execution assumptions and bind one genesis to all prior history. It must establish complete-session capacity and independently reconstruct the entire result, not just closed-trade totals or headline return.

Only after those gates, and fresh explicit authority and registration, should a first experiment use exactly one complete already-exposed train session. Its source, cohort, assumptions, protocol, resources, failure behavior and exclusive fresh attempt path must be frozen **before outcomes**. Previously exposed data stays development research, not an untouched validation set. Expanding to later sessions additionally requires accepted cross-session continuity; no automatic retries, subset scoring, training or promotion follows.

Broker execution/fees/margin and EURUSD spot/CFD mapping remain separate evidence requirements. Futures quote-crossing arithmetic is not broker fill evidence. Nothing in this design changes the BTC/cloud collectors or enables credentials, actual orders, persistent account resume or model training.
