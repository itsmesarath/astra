## Exact bodies and context

The context is `constructed_mbo_json_v1`, exact schema integer 1, and mode `absent` or `present`. Each body retains publisher/instrument IDs, event/receive clocks, order ID, price, size, action, side, flags, channel, delta, sequence, explicit `ts_out`, `rtype` and `record_size`.

Integers remain exact, including values beyond floating-point precision. `instrument_id` has the old U32 domain; declared U64/S64 transport domains and `record_size` 1..65,536 are constructed limits, **not native ABI widths**. Action and side are exact one-character ASCII strings including controls, not semantic enums. No trimming, float conversion, clock normalization or book-validity filter is allowed. Target and foreign bodies receive identical structural checks.

`ts_out` is always a field: exactly `None` under `absent`, or exact S64 integer under `present`. Its mode is never inferred. The raw sixteen-field body digest excludes context and position; interpretation is separately bound by the complete bundle and contextual audit digests.

Canonical bytes use ASCII-escaped, sorted, compact JSON without a newline and with nonfinite values refused. The entire bundle includes context, the entire unchanged old cursor fixture and every payload occurrence. The entire new result includes that bundle, the complete old assessment including its own digest, every audit and every authority; only its own result digest is excluded from its digest body.

## Causal prefix versus complete identity
