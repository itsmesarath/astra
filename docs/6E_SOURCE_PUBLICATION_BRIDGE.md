It retains complete native evidence, all target/foreign raw occurrences, reconstructed book/event/quote/37-feature results, and a separate native-prefix link for each publication.

## Complete identity versus causal identity

The complete association binds original member bytes, native framing/EOF/metadata, all occurrences, the entire adapter assessment and all link records. Its digest changes when any complete member or valid suffix changes.

A causal native chain instead binds the declared context, exact consumed header/record spans and raw/wire projections in order. Each publication links the original first-due adapter cursor to its corresponding consumed native prefix. Future complete-member hashes, frame completion/EOF totals and unconsumed suffix records are excluded from earlier causal links. Foreign records retain their positions and bodies without driving target timers. Wire mapping order is distinct from sorted logical Metadata mapping order.

The bridge preserves daily initialization, reset/event/book/quality/clock rules, full event/T history versus protected execution capture, first equal-time publication, all37 exact features, and complete terminal queues through the unchanged adapter. Unsupported intersection values refuse rather than being relabelled, rounded, thinned, split or silently coerced.

Early refusal guarantees apply to the new outer metadata/pin/context/link boundary. The unchanged native provider and adapter retain their own local verification phases; this composition does not promise a stronger global deep-body gate.
