## What the gate checks

The gate checks whether a complete supplied metadata ledger is internally consistent. This could be one prerequisite for a future separately authorized reader; this component authorizes no richer-data access. It retains excluded, degraded, empty, reconstruction-only and reserved metadata rather than silently dropping them. Selection must be the first prefix of all accepted decision sessions in ledger order, and every selected entry must be train/development. An earlier accepted validation or test session cannot be skipped to reach a later train session. Every selected session's declared publication assertions must be present and ordered.

Every session and bundle is checked before any publication assertion is inspected. Counts, roles, dates, declared UTC windows, identifiers, reference hashes and ordering must agree. Quote-cursor, predecessor-time and completed-event references are checked for consistency under the explicitly declared mapping assumptions. A bad final session cannot be hidden behind earlier valid entries.

The result retains the entire supplied cohort and complete canonical hashes. The verifier rebuilds from the original inputs and independently retained expected hashes, not a submitted summary or previous result. No partial successful cohort is returned.

## What a matching result does not prove

The reference hashes are opaque assertions. The gate opens no source file and recomputes no event prefix or feature vector. It cannot prove that a quote cursor was genuinely first, a decoded event carried its original completion flag, a collection receipt is authentic or CVD, depth, replenishment and absorption were causally derived. Declared dates and UTC windows are not exchange-calendar or contract-expiry verification.

A coherent replacement of all input assertions and expected hashes is not authenticated or detectable. Every authority flag remains false. A matching metadata result does not authorize historical access, training, model promotion or trading.

## Deliberately small scope

This version accepts at most 16 invented inventory entries, three selected sessions and 64 selected publication assertions. These are constructed-test bounds, not historical-session capacity. Do not truncate a real session, select favorable rows, split it into artificial sessions or relabel it synthetic to fit them.

There is no file reader, historical loader, raw quote/event array, price, feature value, outcome, account, fee, margin, model, CLI or order connection. The BTC and cloud collectors remain outside this work.

The next historical layer still needs original completed-event and feature-publication evidence, all 37 causal features, complete-session capacity, calendar support and one-genesis continuity. Any historical experiment requires separate reviewed implementation and fresh prospective registration. Training does not start automatically after metadata checks.
