# 6E trend model v2 (hourly trend + passive entries) — 2025 results (16 Oct 2026)

Code: `src/ai_trader/research/six_e_trend_model.py`. Tests: `tests/test_six_e_trend_model.py`, covering the hold logic, Donchian, refusal of reserved data, an uptrend traded long, the limit requiring trade-through, and back-adjustment.

Results are in `data/processed/6e_trend_model_2025_1.json`.

**Design:**
- **Signals:** hourly Donchian 48 h, momentum 72 h with 1 ATR, and an ensemble of 3 Donchian and 3 momentum lookbacks.
- **Entries:** market orders, or passive limits at the close (offset 0 or 2 ticks, valid 60 min). A limit counts as filled only if price trades through it by 1 tick.
- **Stops:** a trailing ATR stop at 2 or 3 ATR (hourly ATR, median 28 ticks), filled 1 tick worse than the stop or at the gap price.
- **Flat rules:** flat before contract rolls and over the weekend (from Friday 20:00 UTC).
- **Costs:** 1 contract, fees 1 tick per round trip.

**Result:** all 18 configurations lost money, in both April–August and September–December.

| Configuration | Trades | Mean net ticks | Total | Profit factor |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Ensemble, limit offset 2, stop 3 ATR (best) | 205 | −1.74 | −357 | 0.93 |
| Ensemble, market entry, stop 3 ATR | 209 | −2.59 | −542 | 0.90 |
| Momentum 72 h, limit offset 2, stop 3 ATR | 305 | −2.60 | −792 | 0.89 |
| Donchian 48 h, market entry, stop 3 ATR | 208 | −7.35 | −1,528 | 0.80 |

**Diagnosis:**
- 2025 6E trends came with violent hourly whipsaws: April's tariff shock had hourly moves of up to 280 ticks.
- Stops at 2–3 hourly ATR (55–85 ticks) were hit repeatedly before the trends paid out. For the Donchian rule, stop exits averaged −11 ticks and reversal-signal exits −51 ticks.
- Passive entries improved results by about 0.5–1.3 ticks per trade versus market entries. That confirms the cost saving is real, but it is far smaller than the signal losses.

**Conclusion.** After the earlier seconds-scale, minute-model and intraday-rule studies, single-market 6E intraday and hourly trend rules show no edge on 2025.

The best-documented trend approach is different: daily time-series momentum across many futures markets. Testing it needs many years of daily data across a basket of markets (micro contracts make it affordable). A single market over 9 months cannot test it, because the sample is too small and dominated by one regime.

No candidate is frozen. 2026 stays unopened.
