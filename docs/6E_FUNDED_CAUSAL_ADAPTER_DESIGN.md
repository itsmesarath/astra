## Two different completeness claims

The held continuous simulator processes the first native quote at a receive timestamp and decides immediately after that cursor, before a later equal-time suffix. The admission reducer's `complete_through_ns == recorded_at_ns` is a constructed accounting assertion. It cannot establish that all native rows at that timestamp were observed.

A new adapter therefore needs native visibility separate from its accounting frontier. The held `six_e_native_prefix.NativePrefix` now identifies the pinned input/session/dated contract, consumed cursor/count, consumed digest, last receive time and current timestamp's first cursor. Its `PublicationWitness` binds the original first-cursor publication and OPEN boundary; `ClosedNativeBoundary` separately binds supplied timestamp closure. Reconstruct these from independently retained fixture/prefix expectations and the original publication ID or original pending deadline derived from reconstructed accounting, not a submitted certificate or its replacement `through_ns`. Matching digests are local reproducibility, not authenticated feed transport or funding.

Current native, paired-assessment and driver inputs remain guarded by explicit synthetic labels. The native fixture is bounded to 1,024 quotes and 64 publications; the isolated driver narrows this to 128 quotes and exactly one publication. These bounds and synthetic tests are not capacity or calendar evidence for a historical cohort. Historical input/calendar support requires separate reviewed implementation and authority; do not relabel actual sessions as synthetic to bypass the held guards.

Do not wait for a later equal-time suffix before making the original first-cursor decision. Do not make the accounting timestamp a surrogate for that suffix. Any accounting clock advancement must have a defined cadence and its own observed-source witness; inventing a fresh timestamp cannot make stale observations fresh.

Before stamping entry timeout or UNKNOWN at its deadline, consume every eligible native row at that exact deadline. Closure needs a next strictly later receive timestamp or a pinned complete session EOF. The later row can establish the boundary, but its price cannot influence an earlier decision or be used as a deadline fill. UNKNOWN remains terminal after closure, even if a reference or receipt arrives later.

## Insert the gate before pending execution

Apply the original entry-selection rules to the newly recomputed funded-state path at every decision, not only at timestamps selected in the old control. Only a candidate that passes those rules reaches declared admission. Budget denial or undetermined margin creates no pending entry, entry fee, hold timer or exit obligation. Its effect on attempt IDs and cooldown must be specified before outcomes; it must not silently inherit spread-refusal or timeout behavior.

Preserve the original fixed policy and causal feature inputs, one-contract assumption, 100 ms readiness, inclusive one-second execution interval, first-eligible widened-entry refusal, widened exit prices and overshoot, 15-second hold priority at equality, 10-second cooldown and strict end-of-session eligibility. Any changed execution rule would be a different experiment, not a repair of the old control.

Refusing an entry changes later inventory, cooldowns, opportunities and receipts. Recompute the entire registered sequence causally. Removing unaffordable fills from an old completed tape and summing the remainder is not this experiment.

## Bind one declaration across the entire cohort

Preserve negative balances, net realization loss against the original seed, and typed liabilities even when their numeric commitments are zero. Never reset funding by date, session or contract.

The cash buffer is only an admission threshold, not another fee or debit. Entry cash/loss rejection must not suppress a valid exit. UNKNOWN carries the old contract and all commitments; references and new contracts cannot close it. Complete equity and return stay null whenever any liability remains.

Keep the original extra-tick bracket/comparator definition distinct from the new declared monetary accounting. Declared realization uses the permitted crossed entry/exit prices and explicitly declared fees; do not subtract the old extra round-trip tick again. ## Synthetic acceptance before historical authority

- Opposing equal-time rows: the first-row decision is unchanged by an unseen suffix, while later eligible execution can legitimately differ. Wrong cursor/source/digest, omitted or reordered suffix and premature closure refuse.
- Readiness and deadline at one nanosecond either side; a deadline receipt remains eligible before closure, timeout follows closure, and no receipt can close UNKNOWN afterwards.
- A first widened candidate followed by a narrow equal-time quote still refuses entry; hold, cooldown and session-end equalities match the held engine.
- Budget denial changes later eligibility compared with the old inventory-blocked path, demonstrating full recomputation rather than fill filtering.
- Exact cash/loss equality, missing versus declared-zero margin, fees and reserves conserved exactly once, overflow and an exit that retains negative cash.
- UNKNOWN across dates/contracts, including zero-valued commitments, never resets or closes from references.
- Every decision and quote is covered; independent canonical reconstruction and counts match. Capacity failure aborts rather than reporting a successful prefix.
