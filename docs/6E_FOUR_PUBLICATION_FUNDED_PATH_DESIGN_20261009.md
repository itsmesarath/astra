# Next candidate: one complete funded path

This is an unimplemented design only. No source path, simulation, historical input or experiment is released by this document. The current window's roundtrip full-suite acceptance and a separate synthetic liquidity-point increment come first. A dependent central walker needs enough time for reviewed implementation, independent complete oracles, corrections and a fresh frozen-source full suite; do not start it simply to fill the remainder of a four-hour window.

The objective is to recompute every declared publication on one synthetic funded path, not filter previously completed trades. It is a bridge toward a later registered historical experiment, not that experiment, paper trading or autonomous training. Existing historical outcomes, UNKNOWN liabilities and source pins remain unchanged.

## Narrow complete input

Start with one fresh held `NativeFixture`, one original `DeclaredAdmissionGenesis`, one fixed numerical policy and exit plan, and exactly one immutable feature binding for each original native publication. Require 1–4 ordered publications at least one second apart, at most 128 complete quotes and at most 20 accounting records. Retain all input, declaration, individual point and fixed protocol pins. Do not import the historical loader or CPU/Qwen models.

Each binding has the original publication ID, exact caller-declared eligibility boolean and a unique synthetic liquidity point containing all 37 original fields. Validate complete ordered binding metadata and retained-pin syntax before traversing prices or feature values; then verify every complete content pin before any simulated decision. The regenerated publication witness supplies the original first native cursor and time; a caller cannot substitute an EOF prefix or a later equal-time row. Input eligibility and features remain invented assertions. Level-one quotes do not prove order-book depth, trades, CVD, reload or absorption derivation.

Reuse held pure native/accounting primitives and the numerical point selector only after its separate independent and full-suite acceptance. Do not compose complete roundtrip plans: their terminal states have already consumed future prices and cannot be a preceding state for another publication. Keep one central causal walker from the original genesis. Exact output schema, generated identifiers, canonical protocol and byte limit must be frozen in a separately reviewed contract before implementation.

## One cursor and timer ordering

Before a quote at time `now`, close any original entry or exit deadline strictly earlier than `now`. A quote at the inclusive deadline remains eligible. Hold time is actual entry receipt plus 15 seconds; at equality it wins before the quote, using coverage through hold minus one nanosecond. When a gap crosses several deadlines, advance in original chronological order, not directly to the new quote's time or price.

Then consume the quote and its eligible entry, exit or bracket effects. Only then evaluate a publication on that original first cursor, before any later equal-time suffix. Thus an actual refusal or exit at this cursor can cause a cooldown disposition at its publication. The independent vector determines the fixed rule's direction, not an account-independent prediction cached with a future state.

Entry remains 100 ms delayed with an inclusive one-second wait, the first candidate only and permanent widened-spread refusal. Exit remains 100 ms delayed with an inclusive one-second wait, the first crossed price including widening and overshoot, and no funding gate. Time exit and bracket exit retain the original definitions; the extra comparator tick is not another monetary cost. Close original timeouts and UNKNOWN at supplied complete EOF without inventing real elapsed-time or broker evidence.

## Every publication, every disposition

Compute and retain the fixed policy direction at every publication, including blocked ones. Decision precedence is: supplied eligibility and strict `now + 18 seconds < fixture.end`, inventory (including UNKNOWN), pending entry, `now < cooldown`, zero signal, then candidate. Cooldown equality permits a new candidate.

Only a free, eligible, nonzero candidate consumes the next prospective candidate/admission identifier. Consume that ordinal before its budget decision, including denial or undetermined margin. Append a source-linked `ClockAdvanced` and `AdmissionRequested`; snapshot budget from that request's immediately preceding post-clock accounting prefix, not always sequence one or the initial seed. Denial creates no pending entry, fee, hold or cooldown. This funding extension is a newly declared rule, not claimed identical to the old unfunded simulator.

Retain the publication's native prefix and OPEN witness, original point selection, preceding accounting prefix, current cooldown, immutable first disposition, candidate ordinal/ID if any and request budget snapshot. Later refusal, timeout, receipt, hold and exit results belong in a separate complete candidate outcome trace; never rewrite the first disposition with future knowledge.

Original 10-second cooldown starts at actual spread-refusal time, original entry-timeout deadline or actual exit receipt, not publication or requested time. Blocked/no-signal/UNKNOWN observations create no fresh accounting clock. The original caller-declared margin amounts remain immutable; absent margin remains undetermined. The held `DeclaredMargin` has no observation timestamp. Budget snapshot age measures the accounting frontier, not margin freshness, and the candidate's same-time `ClockAdvanced` makes that age zero. Neither this clock nor repeated admission claims establishes or renews a broker margin observation. Timestamped margin provenance would require a separately reviewed future contract.

## One seed, conserved liabilities

Use the complete preceding record sequence and one original seed, fees, USD tick value, margin basis and limits across all candidates. Each receipt charges its entry or exit fee exactly once. Preserve realized losses, negative cash, margin and exit-fee reserves; a cash buffer is only an admission threshold. A valid owned exit is never suppressed by an entry loss or funding ceiling.

UNKNOWN is terminal inventory, including zero-valued commitments. Audit all later quotes and publications without account mutation, late fill, cancellation, reconciliation or reference. Terminal complete equity/return remains null while a liability persists. A resolved flat result is declared arithmetic, not measured broker economics or a profitable strategy.

## Bounds and independent proof

Retain every quote, every original publication, every accounting head, native link and candidate outcome. Rebuild the entire canonical result, not just closed totals. A capacity or pin failure refuses the complete result; no truncation, successful subset, daily reset, reseeding or resumed partial output.

A proposed 256 KiB complete output limit must be measured on 128 quotes, four full five-record roundtrips, maximum identifier shapes and retained point contexts before release. All timer and cooldown arithmetic must be bounded before price access. A separately declared synthetic metadata bound `fixture.end_ns <= native.MAX_INT - 10 seconds` can prevent cooldown overflow; document that scope restriction rather than silently clamp a timestamp or change original eligibility.

Decisive independent tests include:

- Budget denial followed by another candidate where an invented unfunded filled path would still be inventory-blocked: proof of causal recomputation, not old-fill filtering. An initially unaffordable flat seed cannot recover capital merely from improving quotes. Expect another denied request with a fresh ordinal, or a changed no-signal disposition; do not manufacture later affordability or run an old completed historical control.
- Refusal or exit at the same first publication cursor; cooldown one nanosecond before, equality and after; IDs consumed by denial but not blocked/no-signal observations.
- Hold equality before a row, original inclusive deadline rows, equal-time suffixes, long gaps and complete EOF.
- Two or four complete roundtrips with manual crossed-price, fee, reserve, original-seed loss and full head/link/audit reconstruction.
- A later original-seed loss ceiling or negative cash cannot suppress an owned exit. The original declared margin cannot change, and missing margin remains undetermined; do not invent a stale-margin check absent an observation timestamp. UNKNOWN followed by favorable prices and signals produces no further accounting event.
- Changed future points or quotes change complete input/output pins while leaving earlier consumed dispositions unchanged.
- Full 128/4/20 coverage, exact canonical-body oracles, malformed metadata/pins before numeric access, overflow and complete-cap refusal.
