The [held intake contract](6E_LOCAL_MEMBER_INTAKE_CONTRACT_20261014.md) remains authoritative. One independently invented regular leaf must be opened below a registered root, admitted using observations of the opened objects, captured completely through the same handle, and cleaned up before any later bridge call. Complete byte pins precede setup; setup-realized identities and the expected request binding precede capture. The existing accepted bridge and all earlier evidence remain unchanged.

Selected candidate: **synchronous `NtCreateFile`, one component per handle-relative open, holding the volume-root directory and every ancestor directory until capture cleanup**. Every held directory and the leaf use **`FILE_SHARE_READ` only**. The earlier possible `READ | WRITE` directory sharing is rejected for this candidate because it would allow a category of competing access that contract section6 requires the strategy to exclude. Strict sharing may make some ordinary roots unusable; that is an unobserved operability question and a possible refusal, not grounds for silently adding write/delete sharing.

An incomplete close/cleanup outcome gives no positive capture and no bridge dispatch. A failed query/open, inconsistent observation, unexpected status, read/EOF mismatch, timeout, malformed output or cleanup failure must terminate the positive path. Retain full diagnostic attempt evidence under the registered harness; do not return a partially positive public capture. Fixture creation, mutator actions and any authorized fixture cleanup belong only to the prospectively registered proof harness. ## Limits of the proposed claim and closure work

Held object handles can bind subsequent relative opens and reads to admitted objects. They do not themselves prove immutable path text, absence of all past/transient aliases, immutable attributes, immutable storage or vendor provenance. QueryDosDevice returns observations; a changed-then-restored mapping can evade pre/post equality, while already held handles still refer to their admitted objects. Exact original/observed names and IDs may remain equal despite changes outside the observation points. The contract explicitly permits no theorem that matching hashes, IDs or timestamps prove bytes were never modified.

The candidate does not claim that every data-write/delete/metadata manipulation is prevented by `FILE_SHARE_READ`, that a successful “no recall” option prevents all filter activity, that DISK proves locality, that `NTFS` authenticates a filesystem implementation, or that GUID/FileId information is everlasting identity. Those are the reasons for independent controls and conservative refusals. Kernel/administrator compromise, hostile drivers and coherent replacement of every supplied context/pin/receipt remain outside detection. All authority flags remain literalFalse; no historical reading, market data, account, trading, training, scoring or promotion authority follows.
