# 6E minute-level direction baselines — 2025 walk-forward (16 Oct 2026)

Roadmap §8, steps 2–4. Code: `src/ai_trader/research/six_e_minute_model.py`. Tests: `tests/test_six_e_minute_model.py`, which check that features are causal, that labels are aligned, and that simulated trades never overlap.

The run took about 5 s on the linked machine. Results are in `data/processed/6e_minute_model_2025_1.json`.

**Setup:**
- **Data:** 238 days of 2025 one-minute trade bars from the horizon census.
- **Decision rows:** 129,063 in total, one per traded minute from 06:00 to 17:00 UTC.
- **Features (19, causal):**
  - returns over 1, 5, 15, 30, 60 and 240 minutes;
  - realized variation and range over 15 and 60 minutes;
  - log volume over 1, 15 and 60 minutes;
  - buy/sell imbalance over 5, 15 and 60 minutes;
  - distance from the 60-minute mean;
  - time of day.
- **Labels:** forward change in ticks at 15, 30 and 60 minutes.
- **Walk-forward:** a ridge regression is refit on all earlier months, and each month from June to December is tested once.
- **Trades:** non-overlapping, with a round-trip cost of 2 or 3 ticks.
- **Rules compared:**
  - ridge, trading only its top 10% of predictions by size (threshold set on training data);
  - ridge, trading whenever the prediction exceeds the cost;
  - momentum (sign of the 60-minute return);
  - reversal (opposite sign of the 15-minute return);
  - always long;
  - always short.

**Results** (pooled June–December, cost 2 ticks; average ticks per trade before cost in brackets):

| Horizon | Ridge top 10% | Ridge > cost | Momentum r60 | Reversal r15 | Always long |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 min | −2.35 (−0.35) | −1.78 (+0.22) | −2.02 (−0.02) | −1.86 (+0.14) | −1.91 (+0.09) |
| 30 min | −1.95 (+0.05) | −2.14 (−0.14) | −2.00 (0.00) | −1.58 (+0.42) | −1.81 (+0.19) |
| 60 min | −2.35 (−0.35) | −3.51 (−1.51) | −2.13 (−0.13) | −2.25 (−0.25) | −1.62 (+0.38) |

The out-of-sample information coefficient (prediction vs. outcome) by month ranged from −0.095 to +0.097, with no stable sign.

**Decile scan** (bucket cut-offs from April–August, checked again in September–December):
- Very negative 5-minute returns were followed by about +1 tick of bounce in both periods. That is real but smaller than the cost.
- The highest-volatility decile showed positive drift: +1.7 / +6.3 ticks over 30 minutes. This most likely reflects the 2025 EUR/USD up-trend concentrated in volatile sessions (the "always long" rule was also positive gross). It is a trend artifact, not a reliable signal.
- The average 60-minute drift by hour flips sign between the two periods, except at 12:00–13:00 UTC (US data releases).

**Conclusion.** Combined with the earlier October studies:
- 6E's own trade, volume and order-flow history does not predict direction at 15–60 minutes well enough to beat a 2–3-tick cost.
- A bigger model on the same information is unlikely to change that. The gross edges found are at most about 0.4 tick.
- No candidate is frozen, and 2026 stays unopened.
